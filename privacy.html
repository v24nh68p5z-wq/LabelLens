Privacy Policy

Last updated August 11, 2026

Label Lens is developed by Alex, based in the United States. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it's used.

Camera

Label Lens uses your camera to scan barcodes and ingredient labels. Scanning happens entirely on your device using Apple's built-in scanning technology. Camera images are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Barcode Lookups

When you scan a product barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open, third-party grocery database, to retrieve the product's name and ingredients. No personal information is included in that request.

Scan History

Your scan history is stored locally on your device only. Label Lens does not operate a server that stores your scans.

Profile Information

If you enter an email or dietary preferences during setup, that stays on your device and is used only to personalize the app. It is not sent to us or any third party.

Subscriptions and Payment

Label Lens offers an optional Pro subscription. All payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

If enabled, Label Lens may send a local reminder before your free trial ends. These are generated and delivered entirely on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share information with third parties beyond the anonymous barcode lookups described above.

Children

Label Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use after changes means you accept the update.
